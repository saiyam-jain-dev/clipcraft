YOUR LOCAL STUDIO

Engine & settings

Local clipping, no ClipCraft subscription. Optional AI providers improve moment selection; their free tiers have quotas and may require billing. Audio and heuristic selection are weaker than AI, especially for visual-only footage.

Video processing stays on this machine. With an AI provider enabled, transcript text is sent to that provider. API keys are stored by your local server.

Loading settings…

Extra fallback AI engines (IteraCompute, OVH, Kilo, local gateways) are tried automatically when the primary engine fails. They are configured in settings.json under "fallback_providers" and are never shown in the browser.

Leave the key blank to keep an existing key. Gemini free-tier availability and rate limits depend on your account and model.

Groq offers a rate-limited free tier. You can also use a compatible local endpoint. Blank keys preserve saved keys; local endpoints may not need one.

Larger Whisper models need more memory and an initial model download. Optional browser cookies use your local browser session for permitted downloads.
